Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. About the app

"Hon Da – Da kenh" is an internal content-management tool operated by Hồn Đá – Tinh Hoa Đá Mỹ Nghệ (Vietnam). It is used only by authorised Hon Da staff to manage social media accounts owned by Hon Da. It is not offered to outside users and nobody else can sign up.

2. Permitted use

  • Publish only content produced by Hon Da or that Hon Da is licensed to use.
  • Connect only accounts owned by Hon Da, through each platform's official login (OAuth).
  • Follow the terms and community guidelines of every platform (Meta, Google/YouTube, TikTok).

3. Not allowed

  • No copyright-infringing, false or unlawful content.
  • No bulk advertising messages or spam.
  • No use of the app to access accounts that do not belong to Hon Da.

4. Content and approval

Every post is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published. AI-assisted content is labelled where the platform requires it. Replies to customers are sent only when a staff member presses Send.

5. Data

Data is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.

6. Responsibility and changes

Hon Da is responsible for the content posted on its own accounts. The app is provided "as is" for internal use and may be changed or discontinued at any time. These terms may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
