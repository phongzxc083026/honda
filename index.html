Hồn Đá – Đa kênh

Hồn Đá – Tinh Hoa Đá Mỹ Nghệ is a Vietnamese stone-craft workshop that builds family stone tombs, stone gates, incense burners and decorative stonework.

"Hon Da – Da kenh" is our internal desktop application. Authorised Hon Da staff use it to publish videos we produce ourselves to social media accounts we own (Facebook Pages, YouTube channels, TikTok accounts), to read comments and messages on those accounts, and to reply to customers. Every post is approved by a person before publishing, and replies are sent only when a staff member presses Send. The app is not offered to the public.
