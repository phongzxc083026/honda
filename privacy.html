Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Who we are

Hồn Đá – Tinh Hoa Đá Mỹ Nghệ operates "Hon Da – Da kenh", an internal tool used by Hon Da staff to manage Hon Da's own Facebook Pages, YouTube channels and TikTok accounts.

2. Data we access

Only through the platforms' official APIs and only for accounts we own and have connected ourselves:

  • our own posts and videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, shares);
  • comments on our posts and messages sent to our accounts, including the public name and text provided by the platform;
  • basic account information needed to publish (account id, display name, avatar).

We do not access accounts we do not own, and we do not buy or collect data from other sources.

3. How we use it

  • to publish our own content and see how it performs;
  • to read and answer customer comments and messages (replies are sent only when a staff member presses Send);
  • to understand common customer questions, counted by topic without names or phone numbers.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in a local database on Hon Da's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally, never shared and never written to logs. Data is kept only as long as needed for customer care and reporting, at most 24 months.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share platform data with third parties. If a customer asks us to contact them, our staff use the contact details the customer provided for that purpose only.

6. Data deletion

You can ask us to delete any of your data held by Hon Da by messaging our Facebook Page or emailing phongzxc083026@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion / Xóa dữ liệu". We delete it within 30 days. You can also delete your own comments on the platform at any time.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: hotline 0854 783 333, email phongzxc083026@gmail.com.
